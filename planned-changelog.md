## [3.0.0] - 2027

This update will expand the mod beyond just the Shop. The Shop will remain as the entrypoint, though.
This section is subject to change.

### Additions

- Add the building map.
    - (Not scale accurate)
    - This map allows access to other floors of the building, as long as you're actually permitted to go to them.
- Add The Regal Room. (name will probably change)
    - Access requires 10 unique pins to be unlocked.
- Add the Awards to the Regal Room.
    - You can claim some bonuses based on certain number milestones for things found on the Stats sheet.
    - FunkBucks, Melody Stones, and Boxes obtained as rewards don't count towards their respective Awards.
    - Other mods are able to add their own Award categories with their own requirements.
        - They're also able to choose to give no rewards, in the event they should be cosmetic only.
    - Just think of them as achievements but named differently, not to be confused with "Medals" which is what base game (and Newgrounds as a whole) use.

This update will be released after 0.9.0, bringing the mod to the latest version of FNF.
This section is subject to change.

### Additions

- Add 10 new pins:
    - 4 Rare
    - 4 Epic
    - 2 Legendary
    - (More will probably be added)
